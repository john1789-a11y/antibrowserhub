# Vendor pricing and eligibility review, 26 November 2026

This review covers the 13 products in the site catalog. Sources are public vendor pricing tables and help articles. Prices are standard monthly charges unless labeled as prepayment or annual equivalents. Historical editorial scores and affiliate destinations are preserved.

## hidemyacc

The Base plan card and API documentation differ. The site lists API access for Team and Business. [Account API requirements](https://docs.hidemyacc.com/13.-hidemyacc-3.0-api/13.2.-user-api/query-account-information)

The 7-day Starter trial includes 30 profiles. Mini includes 5 profiles. Profile sharing starts with Base, and API access is included with Team and Business. Mini costs $10 with monthly billing, or the equivalent of $5 per month when paid annually. Starter costs $29 per month for 30 profiles with monthly billing.

Mini and Starter are separate tiers. The seven-day trial applies to Starter.

[Official pricing](https://hidemyacc.com/pricing) · [Official documentation 1](https://docs.hidemyacc.com/1.-introduction-and-installation/1.1.-what-is-hidemyacc) · [Official documentation 2](https://docs.hidemyacc.com/13.-hidemyacc-3.0-api/13.2.-user-api/query-account-information)

## undetectable

The pricing table and help article disagree on adding users to Base. Eligibility remains unconfirmed.

Free includes 5 cloud profiles, 50 profile creations per month and 10 browser configurations. Unlimited local profiles and Local API access start with Base. Monthly billing starts at $49 for Base (50 cloud profiles), $99 for Professional (100) and $199 for Custom (200). The pricing page lists annual prepayment separately.

The old free-plan guide also attributed unlimited local storage to Free.

[Official pricing](https://undetectable.io/pricing/) · [Official documentation 1](https://docs.undetectable.io/pay-and-subscriptions/plans-overview/)

## lalicat

Lalicat offers a three-day trial for new users. Personal, Solo, Team and Scale include 100, 200, 500 and 3,000 saved profiles and 1, 5, 10 and 20 subaccounts respectively. Each includes the local REST API and CLI. Personal costs $59 when paid monthly or $531 prepaid for one year ($44.25 per month equivalent). Solo, Team and Scale cost $99, $209 and $499 on monthly billing.

The custom quote and profile allowance are unpublished.

[Official pricing](https://www.lalicat.com/pricing) · [Official documentation 1](https://www.lalicat.com/account-registration-2)

## marketer-browser

MarketerBrowser advertises GO as free with unlimited profiles. Its installer is coming soon. Paid editions include unlimited profiles. Ultimate and Full include the advanced API and cloud profiles. Pro, Ultimate and Full cost $29, $49 and $69 on monthly billing. Annual totals are $179, $299 and $419 respectively. Additional users cost extra.

The vendor advertises GO, but marks its installer as coming soon. Its advanced feature entitlements remain unverified.

[Official pricing](https://www.marketerbrowser.com/en/pricing/) · [Official documentation 1](https://www.marketerbrowser.com/en/go/)

## morelogin

MoreLogin offers 2 free browser profiles and 2 users. Pro starts at 10 profiles. Chrome and Firefox, synchronization and Local API are available. Cloud-phone Open/Local API includes remote connections and lifecycle operations; phone resources are billed separately. Browser RPA requires a local Chromium profile and desktop client v2.73.0 or later. Monthly $9; annual equivalent $5.40/month, billed annually. Cloud phones are separate.

The help article establishes the supported local browser and client version. Entitlement by subscription tier remains unconfirmed.

[Official pricing](https://www.morelogin.com/pricing) · [Official documentation 1](https://guide.morelogin.com/browser-rpa/overview) · [Official documentation 2](https://guide.morelogin.com/api-reference/appendix/changelog)

## adspower

AdsPower offers 2 free profiles. Professional supports 10, 20, 50 or 100 profiles; Business supports larger configurations. It provides browser automation and an AI Agent. September release notes add scheduled Agent tasks, quick profile creation and parallel Agents. Free excludes the Local API and includes zero AI Agent credits. The Local API allows 120 requests/min on Professional, 300 on Business and 600 on Enterprise. USD, no additional members; monthly billing every 30 days. Professional 10: $64.80/year ($5.40/month equivalent).

The published tiers and annual discount support the listed prices. The interactive Professional quote did not load. Business 200 has an explicit $61 example.

[Official pricing](https://www.adspower.com/pricing) · [Official documentation 1](https://www.adspower.com/release)

## gologin

GoLogin Free allows 3 saved profiles and 25 creations per calendar month. Cloud launches and REST API access require a paid plan. Professional starts at 10 profiles and includes one concurrent cloud session and 300 API requests per minute. Monthly prices; annual equivalent starts at $4.50/month, billed annually.

Current support articles supersede the previous cloud and REST API thresholds. Exact SDK, MCP and cookie import/export thresholds remain unverified.

## multilogin

Multilogin Free includes 5 cloud profiles in total, including one mobile profile, with one-time allowances of 200 MB of proxy traffic and 30 mobile minutes. Free profiles are deleted after 7 days of complete inactivity. API access starts with Pro 10. Pro 10/20/50 allow 50/75/100 requests per minute. Business starts at 100 profiles and includes unlimited team seats. Pro 10: $11 monthly or $85/year (about $7.08/month). Free profiles are cloud-only, without API.

The free total includes the mobile profile. The traffic and mobile-minute allowances are one-time grants.

[Official pricing](https://multilogin.com/pricing/) · [Official documentation 1](https://multilogin.com/release-notes/) · [Official documentation 2](https://multilogin.com/help/subscription-plan-comparison/)

## incogniton

Incogniton’s free Starter plan includes 10 profiles for the first two months, then 3. Free built-in proxies are available on every plan. Selenium, Puppeteer, the REST API and the Synchronizer require Entrepreneur or higher. With monthly billing, Starter Plus costs $19.99 for 10 profiles and Entrepreneur costs $29.99 for 50. Standard prepayment discounts are 30% for six months and 40% for a year.

The pricing matrix and account-registration guide specify the introductory allowance and paid automation eligibility.

[Official pricing](https://incogniton.com/pricing/) · [Official documentation 1](https://docs.incogniton.com/getting-started/create-an-account)

## dolphin-anty

Dolphin Anty Free for new accounts allows up to 3 saved profiles, all 3 can run at once, and up to 20 profile creations per calendar month. Starter begins at 20 profiles and adds cloud sync, transfer and cookie export. Additional seats on Base/Team/Enterprise cost $10/$20/$25 respectively. Monthly base prices. Six-month prepayment: 20% off; twelve-month: 40% off. Extra seats cost more.

Starter 20 remains $10/month. A second pass removed an unattributed 10-profile claim under the Dolphin Anty heading of the English free-plan guide.

[Official pricing](https://dolphin-anty.com/tarifs/) · [Official documentation 1](https://docs.dolphin-anty.com/en/personal-cabinet/plans-in-dolphin-anty)

## kameleo

Kameleo Free includes 100 cloud profiles, unlimited local profiles, 2 concurrent browsers and 300 browser minutes per month. Paid tiers include 10, 100 and 1,000 concurrent browsers respectively. Docker and mobile browser emulation are available on Free. Startup, Business and Enterprise cost €59, €299 and €1,499 per month. With annual billing, the monthly equivalents are €45, €225 and €1,125.

The comparison now distinguishes saved and concurrent profiles.
